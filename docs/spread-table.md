# Phase 03 spread table

## Model

`domain/spread` is pure TypeScript.

- `Spread`: `id`, an optional `templateId` (the template it came from, dropped once positions are added or removed), an optional `mainQuestion` (0–300 characters) and its `positions`, always sorted by `index`.
- `SpreadPosition`: `id`, `index`, `question`, `x`, `y`, `rotation` and an optional `drawResult`.
  - `index` runs exactly `1..n` with no gaps or repeats.
  - `x`/`y` are table-relative centre coordinates in `[0, 1]`, independent of screen size and zoom.
  - `rotation` is whole degrees, normalised to `[0, 360)`.
  - `drawResult` is the Phase 02 `DrawResult`: a primary card plus an optional jumper. Both belong to the same position and number.

Character counts use code points (`Array.from`), so CJK characters and emoji count as one each.

| Removing                | The gap closes in the existing order (`3, 4, 5 → 2, 3, 4`) and the change is announced. Positions that hold cards cannot be removed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Reordering              | Moving a position to number _k_ shifts the others to keep `1..n`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Shuffling and choosing  | Everything happens on the table, which steps back to 70% while cards are drawn and shows no position frames or numbers outside arranging. The person presses and holds anywhere on the table, positions included (or the space bar on the pack in the middle) to send its cards, one after another, into a golden vortex over the cloth, the pack thinning until it is gone; a tap or Enter shuffles one round. Letting go, after a hold or a tap, floats every card slowly down flat onto the middle of the cloth, gathers them in a loose heap at the bottom edge, and the wheel opens out of it: every remaining card face down, full size with most of it showing on a flat arc; a sideways drag turns it. A tap slides a card forward a fifth of its height; a second tap on it, or a second Enter, draws it into the next empty position, in number order. Tapping another card lifts that one instead. A chosen card may bring the card next to it as a jumper, at the fixed rate. Shuffling again waits until every position has its card; then the rest of the deck leaves the table and it comes close again. The person then reveals the cards one by one, in any order. Positions added later get their cards the same way. See [ADR 0005](decisions/0005-wash-and-choose-on-the-table.md) and [ADR 0009](decisions/0009-tap-twice-to-draw.md). |
| Moving a drawn position | Allowed. The cards travel with it; nothing is redrawn.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Applying a template     | Only before the first draw. Afterwards use 「清除本次牌陣」 (the ↺ button). Saving and deleting templates is always allowed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

The draw machine draws the card the person chose with `drawAt(index)`; the jumper is the card next to it (`jumperNeighbourIndex`). `continueDrawing()` (`revealed → ready`) readies the next choice from the same shuffle. The order is decided in `completeShuffle()`, when the person lets go of the deck. `alreadyDrawnCardIds` and `previousDrawCount` rebuild the pile without the cards already on the table and without reusing instance IDs.

## Template library

Templates store a layout: each position's question, coordinates and rotation. They never store cards or the main question.

| Built-in | Positions | Layout                                                                                                                           |
| -------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 經典三張 | 3         | Past, present, future in a row                                                                                                   |
| 單張指引 | 1         | One card in the middle that answers the main question itself (「這個問題的答案是什麼？」); every position still has its question |
| 五張十字 | 5         | Centre, then left, right, top and bottom                                                                                         |
| 圓環     | 8         | Clockwise from twelve o'clock                                                                                                    |

- Every spread in the library, built-in or saved, is a chapter of the book; its chapter opens with the table laid out for it ([ADR 0010](decisions/0010-the-site-is-a-book.md)). Saving, deleting, applying another template and the blank table stay in 「編輯牌陣」.
- 「儲存目前牌陣」 saves the table under a 1–30 character name. Saving again under the same name updates that template. The library holds up to 30.
- Leaving 「編輯牌陣」 with layout changes that are not saved asks first (「牌陣還沒儲存」, 「確定離開」／「取消」, focus on cancel). The changes are compared with the library's version of the template the table came from, or with the table as it was when editing began when there is none (a blank table, or a template deleted meanwhile). Leaving anyway restores that version and keeps the main question. Applying a template, starting blank or saving sets a new version to compare with. The rules live in `presentation/components/spread/editBaseline.ts` and `matchesLayout` in `domain/spread/templates.ts`.
- Every template can be deleted, built-ins included, after an inline confirmation. Deleting never changes the table.
- 「還原預設範例」 appears when a built-in is missing and puts the missing ones back first, keeping saved ones.
- 「空白牌桌」 starts from an empty table. A fresh reading and 「清除本次牌陣」 start from the chapter's template, else the first template in the library, or a blank table when the library is empty.

The circle uses eight positions because twelve cards on a 3:2 table overlap at readable sizes; blank and saved spreads can still reach twelve.

## Table and interaction

- The table has a fixed 3:2 aspect ratio. Positions map onto an inner area inset by half a card, so a card at `x = 0` or `y = 1` stays fully on the table. Card width depends on the spread (`slotWidthFor` in `boardGeometry.ts`): 21% of the table width (1.5 times the standard 14%) when the spread has room, otherwise the largest width from 21% down to 14% at which no two cards overlap that do not already overlap at the 11% the layouts were drawn for. The classic three cards get 21%, the circle 17% and the five-card cross 14%. That fitting applies in play only: while arranging, every card is 14% (`ARRANGING_SLOT_WIDTH_CQW`), so cards never grow or shrink as positions are dragged; leaving 「編輯牌陣」 fits them to the new layout.
- **Pointer and touch**: drag a position to move it. A press becomes a move after 5 px of travel; holding still instead switches to rotating (below). A finished gesture never also ignites a card or toggles selection.
- **Numbers**: while arranging, each position shows its number as a faint watermark in the middle of its frame, upright whatever its rotation; a position without a question turns its frame and number orange. In play the frames, and so the numbers, are hidden.
- **Keyboard**: each position is an invisible handle over its frame, named 「牌位 N：問題」 for screen readers. Arrow keys or the numeric keypad (8/2/4/6 and diagonals) move 1% of the table, Shift moves 5%, `[` and `]` rotate by 15°, and Enter opens the editor.
- **Rotate by dragging**: press and hold a position for 0.45 s (a dashed dial and the live angle appear, with a short vibration where supported), then drag around it to rotate. The turning-arrow handle on the selected position rotates on drag straight away; a tap on it turns 45°. Angles are whole degrees and snap onto 45° lines within 4°. A press-and-hold never also ignites a card. The editor keeps ↺ 45° / ↻ 45° buttons and an exact degree field.
- **Zoom and pan**: ×1–×2.5 with ctrl/⌘ + wheel or a trackpad pinch, and a two-finger pinch on touch. While arranging, a mouse also gets + and − buttons in the table's top-right corner (`BoardZoomControls`, ×1.25 a press around the middle of the view); they show only where the main pointer is fine and can hover, so touch screens keep pinching. In play there are no zoom buttons. Dragging the empty table pans when zoomed, except before and during a shuffle, when one finger shuffles instead; two fingers still pinch. Zoom is view state only; stored coordinates never change. Until the table is zoomed, vertical swipes on its background still scroll the page.
- **Deck on the table**: `TableDeck` draws the pack, the vortex (`tableVortex.ts`) and the wheel (`cardWheel.ts`), every card the same size for every spread (`DECK_CARD_WIDTH_CQW`, the largest position size; in the air they take each ring's size), changing to the position's size as a chosen card flies there, above the positions but outside the zoomed canvas. `cardFan.ts` keeps every card in its slot until the wheel is put away; a chosen card leaves a gap. Turning the wheel rotates one layer, every card shows the same pre-rendered back (`pnpm thumbnails`), and only the chosen card re-renders or moves.
- **Flight**: a chosen card flies from where it left the wheel; its jumper follows from its neighbour's place. `planFlight` plans each curve in screen space and converts it into the landing slot's rotated, zoomed frame (`toLocalOffset`), so a card lands exactly on its position at any rotation or zoom.
- The reading list shows every position in number order with its question and result. It is also the accessible summary of the table. On a phone it lies below the table. Where the book shows facing pages, the table is on the left page and the question and this list are on the right, and neither scrolls. The reading is a leaf turn away on the pages after them ([ADR 0014](./decisions/0014-a-chapter-on-facing-pages.md)).

## Persistence

The table itself is not stored. Every visit, a reload included, starts from the chapter's template with no main question and a full pile, so an earlier round never reappears ([ADR 0008](./decisions/0008-fresh-table-on-every-visit.md)). A finished round is kept in the reading history instead ([ADR 0006](./decisions/0006-reading-history-on-the-device.md)), and a layout worth keeping is saved to the library.

Earlier versions kept the table under `tarot-atelier:spread-session`. That key is only cleared now (`RetiredTableStorage`), so an old copy does not linger on the device.

- 「清除本次牌陣」, the ↺ button, starts a fresh spread with a full pile. It works at any moment of play, the laid-out wheel included, except while cards are washing or flying.

The library goes through the `TextStorage` port (`SpreadTemplateStorage`), implemented by `infrastructure/persistence/LocalStorageTextStorage`.

The template library lives under `tarot-atelier:spread-templates` (`application/use-cases/templateLibrary.ts`) as `{ "schemaVersion": 2, "templates": [...] }`. Nothing stored means the built-ins. It is written only after the person changes the library, so an emptied library stays empty. v1 → v2 adds the single-card spread, which v1 libraries never had: after 經典三張 if that is still there, otherwise first, and not when a template already has its id or name or the library is full. A v2 library without it had it deleted and stays so. Unreadable data is removed and the built-ins return with a notice.

Each browser keeps its own reading and library, so people sharing a link never see each other's data. Questions are stored only in that browser's `localStorage`. Nothing is sent to a server or logged.
